Image rules and automatic safety checks＋

Upload screenshots from Once Human. Explicit nudity and sexual content are not allowed.

Before a new preset is published, or an image is replaced through Edit preset, the new showcase image and any new QR/share image are sent to OpenAI’s Moderation API for an automated sexual-content check. Images that are flagged are rejected before they are stored publicly or published.

Revealing game outfits may pass, but the filter cannot reliably distinguish game imagery from real photos. It can occasionally reject a legitimate screenshot or miss inappropriate content. If your image is rejected, try a less suggestive screenshot. Please report inappropriate presets that appear in the gallery.

If the safety-check service is unavailable, the upload is paused and you will be asked to retry later.

Fix a mistake or delete your upload＋
  1. 1

    Open your own preset

    ▧
    Your presetSame browser + saved browser data

    Use the same browser you used to upload it. Click your preset’s picture.

  2. 2

    Click Edit preset

    ✎
    Edit presetCorrect your preset details, or delete your preset

    You can edit the title, creator name, body type, tags, description, Appearance Code and both images. Leave replacement file fields empty to keep your images. Replacement images are sent to OpenAI for safety checking. Complete the security check and click Save changes. Edits have their own allowance: up to 10 per hour and 30 per day in this browser. Editing does not use your upload allowance. Shared connection and site-wide limits also apply.

    While a competition entry has votes, its images and Appearance Code are locked and it cannot be withdrawn. You can still correct its title, creator name, body type, tags and description. Ordinary gallery votes do not lock editing.

    The edit controls also let you permanently delete your preset. Changing browsers or clearing browser data removes access to those controls.

  3. 3

    Keep your browser data

    ⌂
    Ownership saved automaticallyNo account or manual token needed

    Your browser automatically saves a private ownership token when you publish. You do not have to enter a token. Another browser cannot claim your preset. Clearing site data or using another device may remove your access to edit/delete.

Something not working?

If a security check fails, retry it. If the site says you have made too many requests, wait before trying again. Older uploads without a saved ownership token may not show an Edit button.
