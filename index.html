FAQ & Guide

Pick what you want to do. Follow the numbered pictures.

New here? Open “Use a preset you like” first.
The pictures below are simplified illustrations of the site’s controls.

Use a preset you like＋
  1. 1

    Open a preset

    ▧
    Character pictureClick a preset card

    Click the character picture to see its details.

  2. 2

    Save its import information

    ↓
    Download QR / share imageOr copy the Appearance Code

    If it has a QR/share image, click the download button. If it has an Appearance Code, use the copy button. Some presets have both.

  3. 3

    Import it in Once Human

    ↗
    Saved image → Once HumanOr copied code → Once Human

    Open the game’s appearance import/sharing screen. Use the image import option for your saved QR/share image, or text import for the copied code. Available options can differ by platform and game version.

Upload your own preset＋
  1. 1

    Click Publish Preset

    ＋
    ＋ Publish PresetTop of the page

    Find this button at the top of the site.

  2. 2

    Add a name and body type

    ✎
    Preset Title: My characterCreator Name: Your name · Body Type: Female / Male

    Enter a title and your creator name. Choose Male or Female to match your character.

  3. 3

    Add a way to import your character

    ▧
    QR/share screenshot OR Appearance CodeAt least one is needed

    Upload a real QR/share screenshot from Once Human, or paste a real Appearance Code from the game. You can add both. The site does not generate game codes.

  4. 4

    Choose a cover picture

    ▧
    Custom Showcase ImageJPEG, PNG or WebP · Up to 5 MB per image

    You can upload a separate character picture as the Custom Showcase Image. For a code-only upload, this picture is required. Otherwise, the QR/share image can be used as the cover.

  5. 5

    Publish

    ✓
    Publish DesignWait for “Preset successfully published!”

    Tags and a description are optional. If you want to enter an open competition, tick the competition box. Let the security check finish, then click Publish Design once and wait for the success message.

Fix a mistake or delete your upload＋
  1. 1

    Open your own preset

    ▧
    Your presetSame browser + saved browser data

    Use the same browser you used to upload it. Click your preset’s picture.

  2. 2

    Click Edit preset

    ✎
    Edit presetCorrect the body type, or delete your preset

    You can correct the Male/Female body type. Other preset details cannot currently be edited. The edit controls also let you delete your preset.

  3. 3

    Keep your browser data

    ⌂
    Ownership saved automaticallyNo account or manual token needed

    Your browser automatically saves a private ownership token when you publish. You do not have to enter a token. Another browser cannot claim your preset. Clearing site data or using another device may remove your access to edit/delete.

Vote, save favourites and enter the competition＋
  1. 1

    Vote for a preset

    ↑
    ↑ UpvoteClick the up arrow

    Click the up arrow on a card. Your vote stays after a refresh in the same browser. Gallery votes and competition votes are separate. Browser-based voting does not prove one vote per person.

  2. 2

    Save a favourite

    ☆
    Bookmark → SavedYour saved presets

    Click the bookmark on a card. Click Saved above the gallery to find your selected presets.

  3. 3

    Find competition entries

    ⚑
    CompetitionSelect “Enter this preset in the competition”

    Open the Competition tab. To enter a new upload, tick the competition box when publishing while entries are open. You can also enter your existing preset through Edit preset. Read the organiser’s rules before entering.

Report a problem＋
  1. 1

    Click Report preset

    ⚑
    ⚑ Report presetBelow each preset card

    Find this below the preset card.

  2. 2

    Choose a reason and send

    ✓
    Send reportChoose reason → add details → send

    Choose the reason, optionally add details, finish the security check and click Send report. Reports go privately to the site owner. Reporting does not automatically delete a preset.

Something not working?

If a security check fails, retry it. If the site says you have made too many requests, wait before trying again. Older uploads without a saved ownership token may not show an Edit button.
